Limits

What this demo does not claim

  • Latency and tokens are fixed assumptions, not provider measurements â€” the ratio is the lesson.
  • The seeded model router is a coin with memory, not a real LLM â€” real routers fail less predictably, which is worse.
  • One invariant (tracking number) stands in for a real validation suite.
  • Escalation is modeled as a terminal event, not a queue, a page, or a person.
  • The fuse caps a simulation; production needs timeouts, budgets, and kill switches instead.
